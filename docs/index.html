The wonderfully short import process

Drop a book.
Pick up your iPhone.

On a Mac, iPhone Mirroring turns import into a direct handoff. Open Bookshelf’s receiver, then drag an audiobook from Finder straight onto the mirrored app window. Bookshelf takes it from there.

  1. 1
    Open Add in BookshelfChoose Receive from Computer.
  2. 2
    Open iPhone MirroringKeep Bookshelf visible in the mirrored window.
  3. 3
    Drag in your bookDrop an MP3 or M4B from Finder and watch it arrive.

iPhone Mirroring requires supported Apple hardware and software and is not available in every region. Check Apple’s requirements ↗

↓ Drop from Finder

Not using iPhone Mirroring? Choose from Files on iPhone, or use Bookshelf’s private receiver page over your local network.
