Works from any computer

Open a browser.
Drop in a book.

Open Bookshelf’s private receiver, then visit its local address from a browser on Mac, Windows, Linux, or Chromebook. Drop in your audiobook and it travels straight to your iPhone over your local network.

  1. 1
    Open the receiverIn Bookshelf, choose Add, then Receive from Computer.
  2. 2
    Visit the local pageEnter the address and pairing code in any modern browser.
  3. 3
    Drop in your bookUpload MP3, M4A, M4B, multi-part folders, or ZIP archives.

Fastest on a Mac: skip the browser and drag books from Finder straight onto Bookshelf through iPhone Mirroring. It requires supported Apple hardware and software and is not available in every region. Check Apple’s requirements ↗

↓ Drag from Finder

Also available on iPhone: Files, AirDrop, the share sheet, and ZIP import are always there when they fit better.
